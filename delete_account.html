Delete your Bright Invitations account

This page is for the Bright Invitations app by DEVELOPER NAME exactly as on your Google Play listing · Last updated: DATE

Option 1: inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open Bright Invitations and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings → Delete my account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and data are deleted straight away.

Option 2: by email (if you cannot open the app)

Send an email to SUPPORT EMAIL from the email address you used to register, with the subject "Delete my Bright Invitations account". We will confirm by reply and complete the deletion within 30 days, usually much sooner. You do not need to reinstall the app.

What is deleted

  • Your login (name, email address and password) held by Google Firebase.
  • Your credit records, unlock records and the normalised inbox record held in our database.
  • Drafts and finished designs stored inside the app on your phone (when you delete from inside the app).

What is not deleted

  • Copies of cards you saved to your phone's Gallery or sent to others. These are yours.
  • Short-lived server logs (such as IP address and request time) are removed automatically by our hosting providers after a short period.
  • Purchase records, if you ever bought credits, may be kept as long as tax law requires. Remove this line until paid credits exist.
  • Advertising data held by Google AdMob under Google's own policies. You can manage it at myadcenter.google.com.

Deleting your account cannot be undone, and credits and unlocks are lost.
