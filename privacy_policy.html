1. The short version

  • The text you type into an invitation, your drafts, your favourites and your finished designs stay on your phone. We do not upload them and we cannot see them.
  • We keep a small account record on our servers: your login details (held by Google Firebase), your credits and your unlocks.
  • The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which collects its own data to show and measure ads.
  • We do not sell your data. You can delete your account and its data inside the app at any time.

3. Information that stays on your phone

Invitation text, drafts, favourite templates and finished designs are stored only in the app's storage on your device. When you download a card it is also saved to your Gallery. Uninstalling the app, or deleting your account in the app, removes the app's stored drafts and designs; copies in your Gallery are yours to keep or delete.

4. Why we use your information (legal bases)

  • To provide the app you asked for (account, credits, unlocks, downloads): needed to perform our agreement with you.
  • Security and abuse prevention (limits, one account per inbox, rate limits): our legitimate interest in keeping the service safe and fair.
  • Ads: where the law requires it, only with your consent. This sentence is only true once you have added Google's consent form (UMP) for the EEA/UK, or removed those countries in Play Console.
  • Legal obligations, such as keeping tax records of purchases once credits can be bought.

5. Ads

The app shows rewarded ads from Google AdMob so you can earn credits. Google may use your advertising ID to personalise ads. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's Google settings (Settings → Google → Ads). See how Google uses data in ads.

6. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We use these service providers, who process it on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase (sign-in and the verification and password-reset emails)
  • Supabase (database, server functions and file storage)
  • Cloudflare (delivery of template preview images)
  • Google AdMob (ads; Google acts for its own purposes as well as ours)

We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect the app and its users from fraud or abuse.

7. International transfers

These providers operate globally, so your information may be processed outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, transfers rely on the providers' standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict certain uses, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data-protection authority. To delete your account and data, open Profile → Settings → Delete my account, or follow the steps on our account deletion page. For any other request, email SUPPORT EMAIL; we reply within 30 days.

10. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase. Our server checks your signed login token on every request and our database is not directly reachable from the internet, only through our own server function. Premium artwork is stored privately and released only through short-lived links after an unlock. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your personal information we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

11. Children

The app is not directed to children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, email SUPPORT EMAIL and we will delete it.

12. Cookies

The app does not use cookies. This website serves static pages and sets no cookies.
